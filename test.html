SYSTEM CHECK

1. Firebase connection

Signs in anonymously, then deletes that test identity again.

2. Database rules self-test

Runs every allowed and refused action against your real Firebase project using your teacher account and two or three temporary anonymous students. It creates its own test sessions and deletes them (and the temporary students) at the end. It does not touch any other data and does not log you out of the dashboard.

Run it only after publishing database.rules.json. If the new rules are not published, it stops before writing anything.
